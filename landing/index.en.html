SPAS

A Telegram-first AI first-aid coach for teenagers. 30 scenarios, CPR voice metronome, AED map, one-tap call to 112. Free. No app install.
Open in Telegram How it works

How it works

1. Type /sos

Tap /sos in Telegram and the bot guides you through the protocol step-by-step.

2. Call 112

One tap on «Call 112» dials the unified Russian rescue number.

3. CPR metronome

110 beats per minute keeps your chest compressions on rhythm — no counting in your head.

4. AED map

The bot finds the nearest automated external defibrillator using your location.

30 scenarios in the bot

Critical

Adult / child / infant CPR. Choking. Arterial bleeding. Anaphylaxis. Electric shock. Drowning. Stroke. Heart attack. Carbon-monoxide poisoning.

Urgent

Epilepsy. Fainting. Hypoglycaemia. Hypothermia. Heat stroke. Alcohol poisoning. Fracture. Head trauma. Burns. Asthma. Frostbite. Snake bite.

Minor

Nosebleed. Panic attack. Cuts, bruises, splinters (planned for the next release).

SPAS is an information assistant. It does not replace 112/103. In any life-threatening situation the first action is always to call emergency services.

Open code, open methodology

SPAS is open-source under Apache 2.0. Any school, NGO, or region can fork and run it in under an hour. Methodology and learning content are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
